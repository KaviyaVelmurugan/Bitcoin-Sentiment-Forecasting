# Data and reused code

- Coin Metrics price archive: https://github.com/coinmetrics/data ; CC BY-NC 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ . Raw files and the downloaded license stay in `work/data/`. Transformations and results are described in the final report.
- Alternative.me Fear and Greed: https://alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/ . Source attribution required; exact historical availability is an assumption in this experiment.
- CryptoPulse source: https://github.com/KaviyaVelmurugan/cryptopulse-ai . The reused snapshot's license is retained at `src/CRYPTOPULSE_LICENSE.txt`. Required modules were inspected/copied for integration; this is not a claim that all upstream operations are packaged here.
- SignalGuard: https://github.com/KaviyaVelmurugan/SignalGuard . It informed evaluation and failure-analysis design; its Apple data and trading model were not imported.
- Reference notebook: https://github.com/silvainfm/FinTech-Projects/blob/main/Stock%20Predictor/lstm_stock_predictor_fng.ipynb . It inspired sequence forecasting; the experiment differs in targets, scaling and evaluation.
- NewsAPI local development access: https://newsapi.org/terms and https://newsapi.org/pricing . Publisher content rights are separate. News text and derived processing records remain private until appropriate usage rights are established.

No blanket right to redistribute provider data is granted by this project's code.
